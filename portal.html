IHSAS Student Constitution & Bylaws

ARTICLE I: SCOPE & MISSION

The Union shall operate under the central authority of the IHSAS, aiming to facilitate extracurricular growth, Arabic/English/Malayalam literary enrichment, and local neighborhood outreach camps while preserving academic excellence.

ARTICLE II: CORE EVALUATION SYSTEM

Every affiliated batch (Marjan, Wahda, Suhba, Hira, Safe, Isra, Chirag, Swafwa, Alif, Parwaz) and sub-committee shall submit a detailed record of conducted programs with physical bills, participant attendance logs, and verified advisory signatures to receive Best Standing marks.

ARTICLE III: FINANCIAL COMPLIANCE

Budget expenditures must be backed by official voucher receipt IDs. Unverified spending or failure to submit attendance sheets results in zero score and suspension of local treasury allocations.
